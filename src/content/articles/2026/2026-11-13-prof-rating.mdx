Over the past 25 years, UC Berkeley students have posted more than 60,000 reviews of nearly 3,900 professors to [RateMyProfessors](https://www.ratemyprofessors.com/search/professors/1072?q=*). In the past year, those reviews have been the harshest on record.

The Daily Californian analyzed every public review of a UC Berkeley professor posted to the site from 2001 to November 2026 to see how students rate their instructors, and how that has changed over time.

Across all reviews, the average professor rating was 3.74 out of 5. Nearly half of all reviews gave a professor a perfect 5, while 14.3% gave the lowest possible score of 1. But since 2020, average ratings have fallen almost every year, dropping from 3.81 in 2020 to 3.42 in both 2025 and 2026.

The share of 1-star reviews has nearly doubled. From 2015 to 2019, 12.5% of reviews gave a professor the lowest score. Since the start of 2025, 24.2% have.

The decline comes as more Berkeley students are using the site than ever before. Students posted 5,549 reviews of Berkeley professors in 2025, the most of any year, and more than double the 2,621 posted in 2021.

Among the 10 departments, as categorized on RateMyProfessors, with the most reviews, sociology has ranked first more often than any other. Ranked by the average rating of reviews posted each academic year, sociology came out on top in four of the 11 academic years from 2015-16 to 2025-26, including three straight from 2020-21 through 2022-23. English has been the most consistent, never ranking lower than third.

History led in 2023-24 and 2024-25 but fell to fifth in 2025-26, as its average dropped from 4.12 to 3.40.

The bottom of the list has barely changed. Math or economics ranked last in every academic year, math six times and economics five. Math has never ranked higher than sixth, and economics never higher than seventh. In 2025-26, math reviews averaged 2.54, the lowest of any of the 10 departments in any year since 2015-16.

Political science fell the furthest. Its reviews averaged 4.02 in 2023-24, second among the 10 departments. Two years later, its average had dropped to 2.56, ranking ninth, just ahead of math.

Mathematics is the most reviewed department by a wide margin, with 6,549 reviews across 228 professors. Chemistry, the next highest, has around half as many. Mathematics is also the lowest rated among departments with at least 500 reviews, averaging 3.19. In the past year, math reviews averaged 2.54, and math professors account for four of the 10 lowest-ranked professors with enough reviews to be ranked fairly.

Economics and statistics followed math at the bottom, with averages of 3.30 and 3.33. At the top was physical education, at 4.86, though nearly all reviews are of one instructor. Following it are art history, astronomy and geography, each averaging at least 4.15.

Physical education instructor [Toni Mar](/interactive/profratings?prof=Toni%20Mar) is the highest-rated professor at UC Berkeley. Across 626 reviews — the most of any professor on campus — Mar averaged a 4.88, and approximately 98% of students who answered said they would take Mar's class again.

Rounding out the top five are sociology lecturer [Laleh Behbehanian](/interactive/profratings?prof=Laleh%20Behbehanian), psychology lecturer [Arman Catterson](/interactive/profratings?prof=Arman%20Catterson), theater and dance lecturer [Chris Herold](/interactive/profratings?prof=Chris%20Herold) and [Purin Phanichphant](/interactive/profratings?prof=Purin%20Phanichphant), who teaches design, each with an adjusted rating above 4.67. Computer science lecturer [Peyrin Kao](/interactive/profratings?prof=Peyrin%20Kao) and [Joshua Hug](/interactive/profratings?prof=Josh%20Hug&dept=Engineering), who teaches CS 61B, also ranked in the top 10.

[Howard D'Abrera](/interactive/profratings?prof=Howard%20D%27Abrera) of statistics and [Zvezdelina Stankova](/interactive/profratings?prof=Zvezdelina%20Stankova) of mathematics are the next most reviewed, with 452 and 440 reviews, respectively. [Alexander Paulin](/interactive/profratings?prof=Alexander%20Paulin) of mathematics and [Alex Filippenko](/interactive/profratings?prof=Alex%20Filippenko) of astronomy follow with 361 reviews each, and both averaged above 4.5.

While some professors have hundreds of reviews, most have only a few. The median professor has seven reviews, and 530 professors have just one. Only 68 have 100 or more.
